🎤 Voice Teleprompter

Follow my words: listens to what you say and keeps the current word on the yellow line. Best accuracy, works in Chrome / Edge (desktop and Android). Needs internet.
Scroll while I talk: moves at a steady speed whenever the mic hears your voice, stops when you are quiet. Works in any browser, offline.
Mic access needs HTTPS (GitHub Pages is fine) or localhost.
Keys: Space start/pause, Esc back, + / - font, ↑ ↓ nudge, M mirror, R restart.
